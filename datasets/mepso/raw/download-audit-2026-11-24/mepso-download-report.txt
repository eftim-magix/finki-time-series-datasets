VALIDATION
Each saved file passed ZIP integrity checking, contained xl/workbook.xml, and loaded successfully as an Excel workbook. No cached Excel error cells were found in the inspected price sheets. This checks file/period integrity, not the economic correctness of prices or full hourly completeness. Workbooks were preserved byte-for-byte; formulas were not recalculated.
